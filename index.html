About

I am a PhD student working with Prof. Colm-cille Caulfield and Prof. John Taylor in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics at the University of Cambridge. My research focuses on turbulence and mixing in stratified fluids, combining direct numerical simulations with Lagrangian particle methods to understand transport processes in geophysical flows.

Research Interests

  • Stratified turbulence
  • Turbulent mixing
  • Lagrangian particle methods
  • Geophysical fluid dynamics
  • Direct numerical simulation
